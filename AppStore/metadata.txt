Description
Raster puts your apps in the menu bar, so the Dock can stay hidden.

Click an icon to open the app. If it is already running, its window is brought forward, including when it is minimized or hidden. If the app is in front and a window is visible, the click hides it.

Apps that do not fit are in the menu on the Raster icon. Change the order in Settings. Apps that are not running appear dimmed.

Raster asks for Accessibility so it can see whether a window is minimized. Without that permission the app still opens, but minimized windows cannot be brought forward. Open at Login stays off until you turn it on.

No account. Nothing is sent off the Mac.

Review notes
Raster is a Mac menu-bar utility. There is no account, no login, and no in-app purchase.

The pinned apps appear in the menu bar. Left-click launches an app, or brings its windows forward if it is already running, including when a window is minimized or the app is hidden. Left-click again when that app is frontmost and a window is visible to hide it. Right-click for Hide, Quit, Show in Finder, and Remove from Bar.

The Raster icon at the left end of the row opens Raster’s own menu: apps that did not fit, Settings, Privacy, Open at Login, and Quit.

Accessibility, under System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility, is optional. It is used only on the review Mac to tell whether a window is minimized and to unminimize it. Without it, a click still launches or activates the app. Nothing read through Accessibility is stored or transmitted.

Open at Login is off until it is turned on in the menu or in Settings.

Privacy is in the Raster menu and states that no data is collected or sent off the device.
