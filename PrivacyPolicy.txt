Privacy for Raster

Raster does not collect data, and it sends nothing off this Mac. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third party.

The first time Raster opens, it reads the list of apps in the Dock and stores that list locally, so they can appear in the menu bar. After that, it only stores what you change yourself: which apps are pinned, as a path and a local bookmark, and their order. It does not read the contents of those apps.

Accessibility is used only on this Mac, and only if you allow it. It is used to see whether a window is minimized, to bring it forward, and to know when a click should hide the app. None of that is stored.

When you ask, Raster can open, hide, or quit a pinned app. That happens only from a click or a menu choice.

Open at Login stays off until you turn it on. macOS stores that choice.

The information stays until you remove the apps in Settings or delete Raster. Raster keeps no copy with the developer. Accessibility is revoked in System Settings, under Privacy & Security. Open at Login is turned off in Raster or in System Settings.
